Account & data deletion

Delete your Rig account

You can delete your Rig account and everything backed up to it from inside the app, or from this page if you no longer have it. Either way, the deletion is permanent and happens immediately.

In the app, instantly

Delete it yourself

  1. Open Rig and open the menu.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its backup are deleted from our servers right away.

Without the app

Ask us by email

Email us from the Google account you signed in to Rig with and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll delete it and confirm, normally within a few days.

Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (name and email)Deleted immediately.
Workouts, programs, sessions, sets, notes and commentsDeleted immediately, including ones you'd already deleted in the app.
Custom exercises and saved templatesDeleted immediately.
Settings, age and body weightDeleted immediately.
Heart-rate summaries stored with sessionsDeleted immediately.

Nothing is kept. Workouts Rig already wrote to Health Connect belong to Health Connect on your phone; you can remove them there. A membership is billed by Google Play and doesn't end with the account, so cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

This can't be undone. There's no recovery period, and we can't restore a deleted account.

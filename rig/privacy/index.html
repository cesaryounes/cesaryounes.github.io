Privacy policy

What Rig keeps, and why

Last updated October 19, 2026 Contact: cesar.younes@gmail.com

Who this covers

Rig is a workout logbook and timer for Android and Wear OS, developed by Cesar Younes. This policy explains what Rig collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Rig works fully without an account. Without one, your workouts, programs and history live only on your phone and are never sent to us. An account exists for one reason: so your logbook survives a new phone.

What we collect

DataWhen, and what for
Name and email Only if you sign in with Google. Used to create and identify your account. Rig doesn't use them to contact you.
Your training log Only if you sign in. Workouts, programs, blocks and movements, your sessions (sets, reps, weights, times, rounds), notes, comments, effort ratings, custom exercises and saved templates, backed up so a new phone can restore them.
Heart rate during a session Only if you connect a heart-rate strap, a watch, or Health Connect. Shown live during the workout. With an account, the session's average, maximum and a short heart-rate trace are backed up with that session.
Age and body weight Only if you enter them. Age (stored as a birth year) sets your heart-rate zones; body weight lets a finished workout compare volume to your weight. With an account, they're backed up with your settings.
App settings Units, theme, timer sounds, your active program and similar preferences, backed up if you sign in.
Purchase confirmation If you buy a membership, Google Play handles the payment. Rig only learns which plan is active, never your payment details.
Advertising identifier Used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads to people without a membership. See Advertising.

Rig doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. On Android 11 and older, the system asks for location permission to scan for a Bluetooth heart-rate strap; Rig uses it only for that scan and never reads where you are. Rig has no analytics and no third-party crash reporting.

Health Connect

If you allow it, Rig uses Android Health Connect for two things:

  • Reads heart rate, calories burned and exercise sessions, to show your heart rate during a workout and in its summary.
  • Writes each workout you finish as an exercise session, along with its heart rate, so it appears in your other health apps.
Data from Health Connect is used only to show you your own training. It's never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone for advertising, marketing or data brokering. Rig's use of Health Connect data follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Rig's access at any time in Android's Health Connect settings. Rig then stops reading and writing; your workouts keep logging as normal.

Heart-rate straps and watches

Rig can connect directly to a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, and has a Wear OS app for your watch. Heart rate from a strap goes straight to your phone, and the watch and phone talk to each other through Google's Wear OS connection. That data stays on your devices unless you have an account, in which case only the session summary described above is backed up.

Sharing a workout

When you share a workout or program, Rig builds a link and opens your phone's share sheet. The workout is stored in the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to a server, so Rig doesn't keep a copy. Anyone you send the link to can see that workout. Share images are made on your phone and go wherever you choose to send them.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. It goes only to the services that make Rig work:

  • Supabase hosts the account database and handles sign-in. Your backed-up data is stored there, and each account can reach only its own data.
  • Google: Sign-In (your account), Health Connect (on your phone), Wear OS (phone to watch), Google Play (purchases) and AdMob (ads).

Nothing is shared with other Rig users. There's no feed and no public profile.

Advertising

Without a membership, Rig may show a banner ad from Google AdMob, which can use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Members don't see ads. Your training log and health data are never used for advertising. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings under Privacy › Ads.

Your choices

  • Use Rig without an account. Everything works offline and stays on your phone.
  • Sign out anytime from Account. Your workouts stay on the phone.
  • Delete your account from Account › Delete account, or from this page if you no longer have the app. Your account and everything backed up to it are deleted from our servers immediately.
  • Health Connect, Bluetooth and notifications can each be turned off in Android's settings.
  • Memberships are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

How long we keep it

Backed-up data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete a workout, program or session in the app, the backup keeps it marked as deleted, so your other phones remove it too. Deleting your account removes everything right away, including those: there's no grace period and no copy kept.

Children

Rig isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and the database is set up so an account can only ever read or change its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously and fix problems as soon as we find them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date above changes, and significant changes are announced in the app.

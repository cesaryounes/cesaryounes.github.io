Numzies: Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: only your child's first name (or nickname), age and progress are saved online, in a private family space. No account, no email, no ads, no tracking. Numzies is a maths app for children aged 6 to 9, made by Cesar Younes.

No accounts

There is no sign-up, no email, no password and no date of birth. A first name and an age are optional; they are used only inside the app to greet the child and pick a starting level, and they stay on the phone.

The family space

So that a child's progress is never lost and two parents' phones can show the same thing, each child's first name (or nickname), age and progress (stars, levels, buddies) are saved in a private online family space. There is no account, email or password: a long secret code stays on the parents' phones, and only a one-way hash of it is stored online. Nothing else is collected. The data is used only to keep the phones in step and to restore progress on a new phone; it is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or tracking. A grown-up can stop this and erase the online copy at any time from the parent area (Progress is saved online, then Stop). A grown-up sets this up when adding a child, and is told about it, in the app, before progress is first saved online.

Nothing else is collected or shared

The app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Apart from the family space above, it does not send personal information, progress or usage data to us or to anyone else.

No ads, no chat, no outside links for children

Children cannot talk to anyone or leave the app from inside it. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up check.

Backups and save codes

Progress is saved on the phone. Android may include it in your own Google backup, which you control in the phone's settings. The optional save code is created on the phone and only goes where you paste it.

Notifications

Reminders and weekly summaries are created on the phone and only appear if a grown-up turns them on.

Purchases

Numzies Plus (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) is bought and managed through Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks with Google Play whether Plus is active. Volcano Island is always free, and nothing in the app can be bought with real money by a child: the purchase screen sits behind a grown-up check.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

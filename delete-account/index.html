Account & data deletion

Delete your KeepUp account

No app install required Contact: cesar.younes@gmail.com

You can delete your KeepUp account and all associated data either from inside the app, or from this page if you no longer have the app installed. Both paths result in the same thing: immediate, permanent deletion — not a deactivation, and not a grace period.

Option 1 — from the app (instant)

Fastest

In-app deletion

  1. Open KeepUp and go to Account.
  2. Scroll down and tap Cancel account.
  3. Confirm. Your profile, workout history, and club activity are deleted immediately.

If you own a club, you'll be asked to delete it or hand off ownership first — everyone else in that club keeps their own data either way.

Option 2 — without the app

No install needed

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address on your KeepUp account (or tell us the email/display name your account uses if you no longer have access to that inbox) and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done — normally within a few days.

Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile — name, email, photo, date of birth Deleted immediately and permanently.
Workout history synced from Health Connect Deleted immediately. KeepUp never wrote anything back to Health Connect, so nothing to reverse there.
Points, streaks, level, Estimated Effort history Deleted immediately.
Club membership, nudges sent/received Deleted immediately. Clubmates keep their own data — a club roster simply no longer shows you.
Push notification token Revoked immediately; KeepUp can no longer reach that device.
This can't be undone. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there's no 30-day recovery window and no way for us to restore the account afterward.

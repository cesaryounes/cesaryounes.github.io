Privacy policy

KeepUp

Effective August 16, 2026 Contact: cesar.younes@gmail.com

Who this policy covers

KeepUp is a fitness and accountability app that turns your workouts into points, streaks, and friendly competition with clubs of friends. It's developed by Cesar Younes. This policy explains what information KeepUp collects, why, and what control you have over it.

By creating a KeepUp account, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

KeepUp only asks for what it actually uses. Here's the complete list:

DataSource & purpose
Name, email, profile photo From Google Sign-In, to create and identify your account.
First name, family name, date of birth Entered by you during setup, shown only to you in your own account.
Display name, avatar photo Set by you (or taken from your Google photo); shown on leaderboards and to clubmates.
Workout data — exercise type, duration, calories, heart rate Read from Android Health Connect, with your explicit permission, to calculate your points, streaks, and Estimated Effort score. KeepUp never writes anything back to Health Connect.
Club, streak, and points activity Created by using the app — which clubs you're in, your streak, your rank, nudges you send or receive.
Push notification token Assigned by Firebase Cloud Messaging to your device, used to deliver the notifications you've enabled.
Advertising identifiers Collected by Google AdMob when ads are shown — see Advertising.

KeepUp does not request your location, contacts, microphone, or camera access — photo uploads go through your device's own camera or gallery app, and KeepUp only ever receives the resulting image.

How we use it

  • Calculate your points, XP, level, streaks, and Estimated Effort score from your synced workouts.
  • Show leaderboards and club standings to the people you're actually in a club with.
  • Send the notifications you've opted into — streak reminders, leaderboard changes, nudges from clubmates.
  • Keep your account working — signing you in, remembering your preferences, letting you recover your session.
  • Serve advertising to free accounts, once real ads are enabled (see Advertising).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. It's shared only with the services that make KeepUp work:

  • Supabase — hosts KeepUp's database and handles account authentication.
  • Google — Sign-In (your account), Health Connect (your workout data, read locally on your device), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), and AdMob (advertising).

Other members of a club you're in can see your display name, avatar, level, and activity within that club — that visibility is the point of the app, not a third-party disclosure.

Advertising

Free KeepUp accounts may see advertising served through Google AdMob, which can use an advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Premium accounts don't see ads.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or California, KeepUp asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, in line with applicable law. You can revisit that choice anytime in the app's Settings.

Your choices

  • Notifications — turn any notification type on or off in Settings, at any time.
  • Health Connect access — revoke it anytime in Android's Health Connect settings; KeepUp simply stops syncing new workouts.
  • Delete your account — from Account → Cancel account, or from this page if you no longer have the app. This permanently deletes your profile, workouts, and activity. If you own a club, you'll need to delete or hand it off first.
  • Access or correct your data — most of it is editable directly in the app; for anything else, email us (see Contact).

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Deleting your account removes your profile, workout history, and club activity from our systems right away — it isn't a soft delete or a 30-day grace period, it's immediate.

Children's privacy

KeepUp isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit, and access to KeepUp's database is restricted so accounts can only reach their own data and what they've explicitly shared with a club. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat this seriously and fix issues as soon as we find them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: cesar.younes@gmail.com.
